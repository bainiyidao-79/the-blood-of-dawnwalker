Legendary gear is the top tier of equipment in The Blood of Dawnwalker. Pieces come from boss quests, hidden chests, special merchants and rare mission rewards — and collecting the full set unlocks a dedicated trophy and achievement. This guide lists every known legendary item by slot, where it comes from, and the small requirements that are easy to miss.

## How legendary loot works

- Most legendary pieces are **quest rewards** or **placed chests**, not random drops; a few can be bought once their merchant is unlocked.
- Two long quest chains supply a big share of the list: **A Bulwark Against Darkness** (the Arbiter armor line) and **Forge It Anew** (the sword-reforging line). Both can be started once your Infamy reaches level 4 and you become Notorious.
- Completionists should keep every bandit treasure map they find — several legendaries sit at the end of them.

## Chasing the full set

Plan around the two quest lines first — Arbiter armor covers four slots at once, and Forge It Anew gates both a sword and a merchant. Court activities for Xanthe cover most of the accessories, while the bandit treasure maps (riverbank, rocky falls, Tantar Woods) fill in the stragglers. For how the Arbiter line starts — Infamy level 4, Notorious, and a messenger in Svartrau's market district — pair this guide with our endings breakdown, since the boyars you'll be hunting for gear are the same three whose fates decide the finale.
