Time is The Blood of Dawnwalker's real antagonist. You get 30 in-game days to tear Coen's family out of Brencis's hands, and the clock only moves when you do something that matters — quests, exploration, even some level-ups each burn segments off the calendar. Let the 30 days lapse and the story ends badly. The good news: the game lets you earn extra days, and this guide covers the most reliable method — the Tsarina's Sanzhana favor chain.

## How the timer actually works

A few ground rules make the system legible:

- **Time is explicit.** Actions that cost time show an hourglass icon with a number — that's how many segments the action eats.
- **Each day-night cycle holds 16 segments.** Across a 30-day run, that's 480 segments for everything: story, side quests, gear hunts and detours.
- **Open-world events drain time too.** If you hear a ticking sound and see grayed-out segments appear on the timeline, you've wandered into an event that will charge you for finishing it.
- **Quest estimates are per step.** A journal entry showing "1 segment" means one step costs one segment — a multi-step quest with dialogue choices can charge you several times.

Because the budget can't cover everything, spending days wisely — and adding days where possible — is the core strategy of the whole game.

## Earning an extra day: the Tsarina's chain

The dependable way to push the deadline back is a favor chain run through the Tsarina and her Sanzhana retainers. The structure is simple: finish the prerequisite quest, complete four helper tasks for other Sanzhana, then report back to collect a bonus day.

### Prerequisite: Good Home

Everything starts with **Good Home**. Until it's finished, the helper tasks below won't appear. Once it's done, four Sanzhana requests open up around the valley.

### The four Sanzhana helper tasks

| Task | Where to find it | Reward |
|------|------------------|--------|
| Following the Sound | North-east of the Gravel Gorge shrine | A bundle of silk threads — a material used for the Arbiter armor |
| Sorrow of the Burrow | East of the Mud Cup Tavern shrine | A purple amulet |
| Pocket Dancer | Svartrau city center | At night, a two-story house in the city opens up with supplies to loot |
| Pearly and Precious | Below the Little Bridge shrine | Unlocks the Sanzhani merchant, who sells silk threads and skill books |

One warning from the chain's early stages: when you reach the charcoal burner, do **not** pick the option to help him work — it burns 3 time segments. Buy him a drink instead; it resolves the moment without the cost.

### Collect the reward

Finish all four tasks and **return to the Tsarina** — the extra day only lands after you report back in person. It's an easy step to skip in the flow of a busy day, and the reward is lost if you never circle back.

Helping other Sanzhana along the way also unlocks a one-time letter, an extra small perk for being generous with your favors.

## Why an extra day matters

An extra day is 16 more segments — roughly a full main quest plus a side activity, or a legendary gear detour you'd otherwise skip. Given that the deadline itself is an ending trigger (letting the counter hit zero closes the story with the family executed), a bonus day is both insurance and freedom.
