Gear, skill books, herbs, bribe money — nearly everything in The Blood of Dawnwalker competes for the same purse, and the 30-day clock makes "go earn gold the honest way" expensive advice. There is a faster route. A single armor merchant in Svartrau's lower city sits on an endlessly repeating piece of green gear, and with the right preparation his inventory becomes your mint. Fair warning before the steps: this loop is an exploit, it permanently removes a merchant from the game, and it deserves a save file of its own.

## Preparation — do these three things first

- **Make a manual save.** The loop's first step is a murder you cannot undo. If anything goes sideways, you want a clean rollback point.
- **Empty the stash box at a shrine.** You will be producing more gear than you can sell in one pass, and the stash is where the surplus waits for merchant wallets to refill.
- **Pick up the perk that raises sale prices.** Every percentage point on your sales multiplies across the dozens of items this loop produces.

## The loop, step by step

1. **Kill the armorer.** His shop is in Svartrau's lower city. Take him down first, then deal with the guards inside the house; fleeing customers are not your problem and will not report you mid-loop.
2. **Loot the body — repeatedly.** The corpse carries one green (rare-tier) gear piece that respawns every time you loot it. It never disappears, never degrades, never runs out. Each search-through yields another copy.
3. **Sell to the city's other merchants.** Take the stack to Svartrau's wealthy vendors — the weaponsmith and food merchants are reliable buyers. If a merchant's coin purse runs dry, fast travel away and back, or let in-game time pass; their funds reset.
4. **Bank the overflow.** Once every merchant in the city is broke, keep looting anyway. Stuff the gear into your shrine stash box and sell it off in batches once the city's wallets recover.

## Why this loop works so well

The killer detail is the cost line: none of this — not the kill, not the looting, not the selling — burns time segments. In a game where the calendar is the real enemy, a money printer that charges zero days is effectively a free economy. Run it once early, bank a few thousand gold, and fund your actual build: skill books, potions, and the legendary equipment hunt.

Two last cautions. First, the dead armorer stays dead — if a future patch ties quests or services to him, your save inherits the loss, which is exactly why the manual save comes first. Second, exploits like this tend to get patched; enjoy it while it lasts.
