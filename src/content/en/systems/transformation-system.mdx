The Blood of Dawnwalker has no mounts. What it has instead is Coen himself: once you push deep enough into the Vampirism skill tree, he can shed his human shape and cross the Vale Sangora as a wolf. It is the fastest way to explore the map, and this guide covers exactly how to unlock it, when you can use it, and what to do about the one big limitation — it only works at night.

## How to unlock Shapeshift

The ability is bought with skill points, and any shrine in the valley can teach it:

1. **Walk up to any shrine** — every unlocked shrine opens the same upgrade menu.
2. **Open the Traits tab**, then switch to the **Vampirism** skill tree.
3. **Buy Shapeshift**, the ability in the bottom-right corner of the tree.

Two details from the ability panel are worth internalizing early. First, Shapeshift is flagged **night only** — the icon, the description, everything about it is built around darkness. Second, it "doesn't take up a Common slot in the Active Abilities panel," so learning it never costs you a combat ability slot.

## Using wolf form

With the ability learned, transformation is a single input: **click the left stick (L3) while running** and Coen drops onto four paws. From there, keep the wolf's rules in mind:

- **Night only.** Daylight forces you back on two legs, no matter how much open road is ahead.
- **No fighting in wolf form.** The wolf is a travel form. You cannot attack, and you cannot transform mid-combat — break contact first, then run.
- **Softer landings.** Fall damage is reduced while transformed, which makes wolf travel the safe way to descend the valley's cliffs and ravines.
- **Haste is the point.** Shapeshift grants Haste in exploration, and the tree offers upgrades along three lines: making Haste cost 50% less stamina, making it 20% faster, or removing its stamina cost entirely. The final tier effectively turns the wolf into a free sprint.

## The daytime alternative: Mercurial Fervour

Because the wolf sleeps through the day, daytime travel needs a different answer, and the **Witchcraft** tree has one: **Mercurial Fervour**, a day-only ability that grants the same Haste effect by briefly slowing the world around you. It is also flagged as not taking a common ability slot, and it benefits from the same Haste upgrades on its branch.

The practical setup most runs settle into is simple: Mercurial Fervour for daylight errands, the wolf for night. That pairing pairs naturally with the day-night clock — see our guide to extending the 30-day timer, because every hour you save on travel is an hour you can spend on quests.
