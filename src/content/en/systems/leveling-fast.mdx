Experience in The Blood of Dawnwalker usually costs time — and time is the one resource you cannot earn back. This farming loop is the exception: it converts ordinary NPCs near shrines into a renewable XP source, charges zero time segments, and pays a corruption bonus if you do your hunting after dark.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/guides/leveling-night-ambush.webp" alt="Fighting through a night ambush in The Blood of Dawnwalker" /><figcaption>Night hunts pay twice — normal XP plus corruption XP that pushes your vampiric corruption level upward.</figcaption></div>

## The core loop

1. **Pick a target.** Walk to any shrine and look for ordinary NPCs nearby. Crucially, avoid named vendors and village leaders — weaponsmiths, armorers, elders. Those deaths are permanent, and the loss of their services outlasts any XP gain.
2. **Kill and collect.** Every kill near the shrine grants XP and costs nothing from the 30-day calendar. Kill at night and each kill also pays corruption XP, raising your vampiric corruption level — its own reward if you are building toward the vampire side of the story.
3. **Reset the room.** Once the area is empty, fast travel to any other shrine and travel straight back. Everyone you killed has respawned. Rinse and repeat for as long as your patience holds.

## The best farming spot: Urisha village

Any shrine works, but the densest respawn pool in the valley is the **Urisha village shrine** — it just needs unlocking first, via a short chain in the Svartrau arc:

1. **Follow the blood.** In Svartrau, enter a certain informant's house through the balcony and poke around; the scene triggers an investigation into a bloodstained room. Finishing it points you toward **Lacra**.
2. **Track Lacra down and defeat her**, then question the kidnapped Urishans she was holding. They carry the lead to their home village.
3. **Take the lead and go.** It starts the *Song of the Mountain* thread — follow the guidance to the village.
4. **Mind your manners at the gate.** Talk to the village's matriarch and choose your words carefully; push too hard and the meeting turns hostile. A calm conversation unlocks the Urisha village shrine, and with it, the farming spot.

From that point on, the loop is: kill, fast travel out, fast travel back, kill again. Between sessions you can spend the XP at any shrine — which is exactly where you are already standing.

## Two warnings before you start

- **Not everyone comes back.** Only ordinary NPCs respawn through the fast-travel reset. Named quest givers and merchants do not, and several of them gate gear, services, or entire quest lines.
- **Corruption is a direction, not just a bonus.** Night farming stacks corruption XP, and corruption shapes how Coen's vampiric half develops — including how some story beats and endings read. Farm at night when you want that pull; farm by day when you don't.

Pair this with the fast money loop and a quick read of the 30-day timer mechanics, and your Coen hits the mid-game levels with a full purse and days still on the clock.
