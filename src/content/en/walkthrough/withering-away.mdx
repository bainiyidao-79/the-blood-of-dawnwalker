*Withering Away* is the first real morning of The Blood of Dawnwalker and the quest the prologue most wants you to complete cleanly — do it right and you bank relationship progress with **Anca**, unlock her shrine, and open two follow-up quests (*Page-Turner* and *Like Father, Like Son*). It plays as a chain of small household errands, and nearly every one of them hides a choice.

## The morning, in order

1. **Get dressed.** Open the chest beside your bed for clothes. Grab the weapon and odds and ends to the left of the door on your way out — the house chests hold a few more pickings.
2. **Chores first.** Split the wood in the yard, then light the kitchen stove. The stove triggers the family breakfast cutscene.
3. **The breakfast choice.** Asked how to handle your little sister's appetite, take the gentle line — promising not to force her to eat. It costs nothing and sets the tone of the household scene.
4. **Visit Anca for herbs.** Follow the objective to Anca's hut at the edge of the glen. Arriving here automatically unlocks **Anca's hut shrine** — your first reminder that shrines are where trait and skill points get spent. There is loot in the chest by her house.
5. **The riverside scene.** Anca is washing at the river to the right of her hut. The dialogue chain that follows is the important part — see below.
6. **Back inside for the payment.** Follow her in to settle up for the herbs, then decide how the morning ends.

## The riverside dialogue chain (Anca bond route)

By the river, the bond-building path reads:

1. **Keep watching** rather than look away.
2. **Ask about her scars** — "But what about those scars of yours?"
3. **Apologize for prying** — "Sorry. I shouldn't have asked."
4. **"I'm fine"** to close the scene.

This sequence advances Anca's romance arc; brushing the moment off closes it. She is one of the companions with a full quest line, so this five-minute scene is among the highest-value choices in the prologue.

## The herb payment

Inside the hut you can pay **50 gold** for the herbs or say you're short. Paying pushes her bond up. Promising to settle the debt later also works — as long as you actually pay before you head to the monastery much later in the story, the outcome holds.

## The reading invitation

At the end of the hut scene, the warm answer — "I'd love to, I miss evenings like this too" — spends **2 time segments** and triggers the *Page-Turner* side quest, Anca's poetry date and romance on-ramp. If your build of the morning can spare the segments, take it.

## The homestretch

Walking home, you can unlock the **Laslea Glen center shrine** on the way. At the house you'll overhear Vladimir and Pieter talking at the door. Inside, your father prepares a bath — answer **heat the water**, then **add three spoonfuls of herbs**, then "sounds good," and close with **"Father, if you ever want to talk…"** to end the quest on the warmest available note.
