*From Above* is the quest that opens the map — literally. Climb the watchtower in the Briar Sloughs, scout the valley in three directions, and the game fills your map with points of interest. Pair it with the nearby *A Pilgrimage of Power: Bear Trouble* trial and one tower visit pays for a whole day of planning.

## How to start

Leave Laslea Glen and follow the road down. The **Laslea Gate shrine** on the way is worth unlocking as you pass — it's the anchor for this whole corner of the map. From the gate, the watchtower quest marker pulls you south into the **Briar Sloughs**.

## The watchtower climb

At the tower, clear the enemies around the base, then head up:

1. Inside, use **Shadowstep** to teleport to the upper platform — or hop to the wall first and walk it, if you'd rather practice Planeshift.
2. From the window, Shadowstep across to the far side, then up again to the **tower top**.
3. Loot the **chest** up here before you do anything else.

Now the actual objective: step to the highest point and **scout three areas**. Each scouting ping fills in a wedge of the map with points of interest — shrines, camps, quests. When the third sweep lands, the quest completes. That's the reward in itself: *From Above* turns the valley from a fog bank into a plan.

## Getting down (the fast way)

Exit through the tower door, Shadowstep to the upper wooden platform, jump to the stone wall, and **Clawride** down the face. Faster than the stairs, and worth practicing — the valley is full of towers.

## On the way back: Bear Trouble

West of the Laslea Gate shrine, the cliffs hide the **A Pilgrimage of Power: Bear Trouble** trial, and it chains neatly onto your descent:

1. Cross the river below the tower — the story scene triggers, then climb the rocks above.
2. Mark the **wind chimes** on your map and follow the road up to them.
3. Behind the chimes waits the trial's boss: a **ravenous bear**. It hits like a landslide and telegraphs everything — learn its two-swing combo, punish the recovery, and mind the slope (the bear fights uphill better than you do).
4. After the kill, examine the **knife on the ground** by the marked rock, then the **corpse in the cave** behind it.
5. Return to the marked rock and investigate to earn the **Ravenous Flock manual**, along with the trial's XP and an amulet.

One warning for the descent: if the night is getting old, bank the climb. The bear fight at dawn is a very different fight.
