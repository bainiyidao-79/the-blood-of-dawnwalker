*The Cursed Chasm* is the Briar Sloughs' dungeon quest: a broken shrine that starts the *Echoes of Silenced Bells* thread, a gorge the locals avoid, a mural that shouldn't exist, and a boss that is quite literally an echo of something older. The loot is excellent — two manuals' worth of progression and one of the game's famous swords.

## How to start: the broken shrine

Begin at the **broken shrine on the western slope path**. Interacting with it starts the shrine-repair thread (*Echoes of Silenced Bells* in the quest log). Use **Focus mode** to follow the footprints from the shrine to a camp in the trees; clear the camp, then search it for the **shrine heart** in a chest. Carry it back and repair the shrine — the mended shrine becomes a fast-travel node and the quest's true starting gun.

## Down into the chasm

From the mended shrine, continue to the **Coward's Path shrine** nearby and unlock it too. Follow the road to its end, where the chasm quest formally begins. Then double back a short stretch: on the right you'll find the jump-down into the gorge. Drop in and follow the chasm to its end, where the **mural** waits on the rock.

## The astral resonance challenge

Interacting with the mural opens the **astral resonance challenge** — a timed cancellation mini-game where you match the glowing sigils before the resonance fades. It's the same skill the game has been teaching you at every shrine; read the pattern, don't mash.

Success opens the chest beside the rock: the **Cycle of Destruction manual** and the **Guildmaster's Blade**.

## The boss: Echo

The chasm doesn't let you leave with the loot. On the climb back out, the wall itself answers — **Echo**, a boss fight against a reflected, hollow version of a warrior. Practical notes:

- Echo mimics aggression with aggression — the fight punishes greed harder than most early bosses.
- Keep Shadowstep for the beats where it mirrors your recovery frames; punishing the mirror-copy's long recovery is the safest damage window.
- If you're running low, remember the chasm's rats: Voracious Bite outside combat turns the fauna into a potion.

Beat it and the chest behind it yields **Durandal** — yes, *that* Durandal — a sword well above its pay grade this early. With the boss down and both shrines repaired, the Briar Sloughs' cursed corner is closed out: 250 XP, two manuals' worth of reading, and a legendary blade on Day 2 or 3.
