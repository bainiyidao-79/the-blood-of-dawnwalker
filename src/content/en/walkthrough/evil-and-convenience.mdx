*Evil and Convenience* — the full title is *Ambrus's Dungeons: Evil and Convenience* — is the Ambrus arc's darkest stop: an outpost that is part prison, part torture chamber, run by a warden who would rather buy his life than keep it. It's also the arc's best treasure hunt, if you read the clues the camp leaves lying around.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/guides/evil-and-convenience-camp.webp" alt="The gates of Ambrus's dungeon outpost" /><figcaption>The outpost gate — from the outside a camp, from the inside a ledger of misery.</figcaption></div>

## How to start

Investigate the outpost southwest of the Stoneward Post. Clear the camp's defenders, then put **Focus mode** on everything — this quest is unusually generous with clue objects.

## The evidence trail

- On the box by the pit on the camp's right side: a **torn flag**. Take it.
- In the house on the left: an **envelope** and a **brass key** from the table. The key opens what the envelope describes.
- Follow the **footprints** across the ground to the locked door, and let yourself in with the brass key.

## The warden's bargain

Inside waits the **warden** — the man who runs Ambrus's dungeons and knows exactly how this ends for him. When he offers everything for his life, take him up on it: **"I'll allow you to buy your life."** He pays in information — the location of the camp's hidden treasure. Mark it; you'll walk out through it on the way home.

## The interrogation

The quest's signature scene is next:

1. In the shed deep in the camp, pick up the **torture implements**.
2. Enter the adjoining house and find the body of the man the tools were used on.
3. Cast **astral communication** on the corpse and give it back its things — the **flag** and the **implements**.
4. The dead man talks: what the outpost did, and what it was for.

Necromancy as detective work — the game keeps finding new uses for the spell, and this is the coldest one.

## The last stop

On the way out, check the **withered tree** on the right outside the camp — the warden's treasure is stashed there. Loot it, and the quest completes for **250 XP**, an **Amulet of Endurance**, a potion recipe, and 60 denarii. The dungeon ledger closes; Ambrus's network is another link shorter.
