*On the Run* is the prologue's comedy relief: Iorgu has lost a pig, the pig does not want to be found, and Coen — newly turned creature of the night — gets to spend his afternoon chasing it through the village. Short, cheap, and one of the more memorable half-hours in Laslea Glen.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/guides/on-the-run-pigpen.webp" alt="The pigpen scene during On the Run in The Blood of Dawnwalker" /><figcaption>The famous pigpen scene — bring your patience, and maybe your appetite.</figcaption></div>

## How to start

During the wait before mass, find **Iorgu** near the center of Laslea Glen and talk to him. His pig has bolted, and he needs someone quick to bring it home.

## Find the trail

Follow the objective to the bridge and use **Focus mode** to pick up the trail. Cross the bridge, turn right, and walk to the end of the path — the scene triggers when you catch up with the runaway.

When the moment comes to explain yourself, pick **"The duke said so"** — the Brencis-flavored bluff that moves the scene along.

## The chase

From here the quest is exactly what it sounds like: follow the pig, sprint when it bolts, and stay with it until Coen finally grabs the animal. Carry it back to Iorgu to close the loop.

## The drinking scene

Back at the pen, Iorgu's gratitude turns into a drinking session, and the quest slips from comedy into something stranger:

1. **"All right, I'll help you."**
2. Take the drink: **"All right, pour one."**
3. Soothe the pig: **"There's a good girl. Good girl."**
4. Then, at the moment the scene has been building toward, look away: **"[Turn away and don't look]"**

Yes, the pig gets slaughtered, and no, the game does not let you look away from what follows — the turn-away option is the punchline. Talk to Iorgu one last time, close with **"Thank you,"** and the quest completes for **100 XP and 25 denarii**.

It's a small quest, but the XP-to-time ratio is honest, the tone is a palate cleanser between the prologue's heavier beats, and the village remembers it fondly.
