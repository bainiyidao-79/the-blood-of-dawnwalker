*Good Home* is the doorway to The Blood of Dawnwalker's most valuable reward: finish it and the four Sanzhana helper tasks open up, and finishing those buys you **an extra day** on the 30-day clock. It is also, more quietly, one of the game's best-written hours — a missing child, a charcoal camp, and the strange court of the Sanzhana Tsarina.

## How to start

Come to the **Gravel Gorge shrine**, open the map, and take the quest marker at the gorge's upper right. At the marker, *Good Home* begins — the Tsarina's business, though you don't know her yet. Follow the light-trail onward.

## Haylee, in the cave

The trail ends at a cave, and the cave holds a little girl called **Haylee**, playing games with someone who is not entirely a person. The conversation is a game of answers:

- Open with the white option: **"What game do you want to play?"**
- Answer her riddle with **"a curse"**, then **"question time."**

Keep the exchange going and the trail points onward: she wants you to find the **wooden palace**. That's your next objective — and the quest's whole shape.

## The wooden palace

The palace sits near the **East Road shrine** by the ruins (unlock the shrine as you pass; the ruins also hide a side objective that permanently raises max health, worth the detour if you can spare it):

1. In the village, **Focus-scan the house on the left**.
2. At the wooden palace itself, search **both the ground floor and the upper floor**.
3. Follow the smoke to the **charcoal burners' camp** and talk to their leader — he'll point you toward the man you're looking for.

## The charcoal burner

Find the man at his camp under the tree marks. The classic mistake is offering to help him work — that burns **3 time segments**. The correct social lubricant is cheaper: **"How about I buy you a drink instead?"**, then offer the **Transylvanian white wine**. With the drink done, take **"I just want to help"** and bring him back to Haylee.

## The Tsarina's court

Back at the cave — or rather, through it — you meet the **Sanzhana Tsarina** herself, and the conversation that decides Haylee's future runs: **"The girl should be with her own family."** → **"The real question is what staying here means for her."** → **"All right, no problem."**

Then comes the price. The Tsarina wants **three shiny teeth** — human teeth, taken from the living. Kill soldiers or civilians until you have three, and return them to her at the cave.

## The letter, and the unlock

Submit the teeth, then finish the scene with the letter from your sister: **"Tell her I will get them all out."** The quest closes — and with it, the four Sanzhana helper tasks open across the valley: **Following the Sound**, **Sorrow of the Burrow**, **Pocket Dancer** and **Pearly and Precious**. Finish all four, report back to the Tsarina in person, and the extra day is yours. Our guide to extending the 30-day timer walks that chain in full.
