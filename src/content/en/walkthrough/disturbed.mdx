*Disturbed* is a grave-robbery mystery with a duel in the middle and a wake at the end. Someone has dug up the dead, a grieving woman wants them returned, and Coen's investigation leads somewhere uncomfortably close to home. Expect 100 XP, 25 denarii, and the prologue's best small scene with Vladimir.

## How to start

During the wait before mass, check the map marker near the church and talk to the woman there — the grave of a man named **Kvitko** has been dug open, and she wants him put back properly. Agree to look into it.

## Follow the evidence

1. **Examine the grave with Focus mode**, then follow the footprints into the village.
2. The trail leads to a **shovel under a shed canopy** — examine it.
3. Enter the house the trail points to and sweep it with Focus for clues; the scene triggers a confrontation with the man who lives there.

## The duel

When the householder — no stranger to Coen's family — refuses to talk, the conversation turns into a fistfight. Win it to open the real conversation.

## The wake

After the fight, you'll find **Vladimir** and share a drink. Take the toast: **"To Kvitko."** The follow-ups shape the moment: **"Why didn't you ever tell me this before?"** and **"Your secret is safe with me."** It's a quiet, well-written scene, and the secrets Coen keeps here say a lot about the person he is becoming.

## The reburial

Return to the woman by the church and close the conversation with **"All right."** Then carry Kvitko back to his grave and lay him to rest — the reburial completes the quest and pays out **100 XP and 25 denarii**.

It's a short loop, but it shows off the game's small-scale storytelling: a mystery, a fight, a funeral, and a friend with secrets — all inside a single morning.
