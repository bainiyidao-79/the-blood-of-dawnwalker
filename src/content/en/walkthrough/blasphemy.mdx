*Blasphemy* is the prologue's detective story: someone has stolen a banner that matters to the village, Gremla wants it back, and Coen gets to play investigator with the Focus ability. It's one of the better-rewarded side quests of the opening days — up to 850 XP, 25 denarii, and a **Daylight Amulet** — and a genuinely satisfying little mystery.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/guides/blasphemy-village-gate.webp" alt="The village gate in Laslea Glen where the Blasphemy investigation begins" /><figcaption>The trail starts at the village gate — keep Focus handy, the evidence is painted all over the ground.</figcaption></div>

## How to start

During the wait before mass, find **Gremla** in the middle of Laslea Glen and talk to her. When she fumes about the stolen banner, answer **"Killing us does Brencis no good"** and take the case.

## Investigate the shed

Use **Focus mode** around Gremla's shed and the nearby ground to collect clues — footprints lead away from the shed's left side to a **torn banner fragment**. Grab it; it's the physical evidence the whole case hangs on.

## Work the witness trail

- **The white-haired elder** at the shed door: press him — **"So you know nothing about this?"**
- Follow the objective to the next villager and be straight with him: **"I only want to help."**
- Find the walking crutch by the third villager's spot and talk to her.
- Finally, corner the man who has been watching all of this — when you present your evidence, accuse him directly: **"Why did you steal the banner?"** He folds.

## The verdict scene

The thief walks you to his painting and explains himself: he wanted to defy the vampires. Your options shape the ending of the case:

- **"There are better ways to fight Valexius than this."**
- **"Enraging Brencis endangers my family."** — the protective pick, and the honest one for Coen's situation.
- **"This is just a pointless show. You have to give the banner back."** — the pragmatic closer.

Return the banner to Gremla to complete the quest. The reward scales with how thoroughly you investigated — somewhere between **75 and 850 XP**, plus **25 denarii** and the **Daylight Amulet**, a rare prologue trinket. It also marks a minor story consequence you'll feel in the village's mood.
