*The Firebrand* is Day 1's big errand: a fight at the mill, a plea from a captured man's wife, and a slow, careful infiltration of the Stoneward Post as a volunteer recruit. It ends with Coen holding prison intel — and two follow-up quests that take Mert back out of Brencis's machinery.

## The mill

Follow the marker to the mill and walk straight in. The exchange with the men inside goes sideways on purpose:

- **"I am Coen of Laslea."** — claim your name.
- When they push, **"I was just taking a leak."** — the cheeky answer has its uses.

The smarter play is to walk out and come back in again: the restart gives you an **ambush angle**, and vampiric powers make short work of the group from behind. Clear them all.

## Lanna's plea

With the mill quiet, talk to **the miller's wife**. Apologize — **"I'm sorry, but this had to be done"** — and keep listening: her man **Mert** has been arrested and taken to the Stoneward Post. Promise **"I'll do everything I can to get him out."** Before leaving the mill area, unlock the **Lakeside Mill shrine** on the road west — it's your fast-travel anchor for this whole arc.

## Enlisting at the Stoneward Post

Follow the marker to the **Stoneward Post** and unlock its shrine beside the gate. One timing note: **arrive by day**. Come at night and the guards simply tell you to come back in daylight — a wasted trip the quest never warns you about.

Inside the walls, the infiltration is a conversation chain:

1. Tell the gate guard **"I… I want to enlist!"**
2. When he balks — **"What? I'm a soldier too. How am I supposed to fight without a weapon?"**
3. Mind the **merchant by the path** — you'll need him in a moment.
4. In the tent at the back of the inner yard, find the **commander** and talk your way forward. Follow the marker to the **overseer**, and on the way, talk to the woman on the path: she knows how things get stolen in and out of the prison.
5. Take that knowledge to the **merchant**, then decide: **"Maybe I should just ask the commander directly…"** → **"Don't blame me for this. I'm going to the commander."**
6. **Expose the theft ring** to the commander. That's the move that earns trust — and with it, permission to visit the prisoners.

## The cell visit

The overseer walks you to the cells. Talk to the imprisoned **miller** — Mert himself:

- **"Lanna is worried sick about you."**
- **"Tell me about your… 'friends' in here."**

A guard interlude follows; when the sergeant starts throwing up, cover with **"Commander… he's going to be sick!"** Once the guard is gone, squeeze the miller: **"It was them or you."**, then **"There are too many soldiers here."** — that's the prison layout intel, and the quest completes.

*The Firebrand* ends by handing you its sequel: the jailbreak (**Under Watchful Eyes**) and the outside contact (**Shadows in the Woods**). Before you leave, two small grabs: the **jail key chain** on the left cabinet, and whatever the boxes in the cells are holding. Theft, in this case, is practically quest-sanctioned.
