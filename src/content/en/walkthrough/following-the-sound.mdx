*Following the Sound* is the first of the four Sanzhana helper tasks — the chain that pays an extra day on the game's 30-day clock. A Sanzhana waits in the field, listening to something underground; you are the shovel. Bring a blade for what's making the noise.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/guides/following-the-sound-forest.webp" alt="Following the sound through the woods to the Sanzhana" /><figcaption>The sound in the trees — the Sanzhana hears it before you do.</figcaption></div>

## How to start

Begin at the **Mud Cup Tavern shrine** and follow the quest marker into the fields. You'll find the **Sanzhana** standing in the open, head tilted toward the ground. Talk to it, and it leads you to the **burrow** — a den in the hills where something has been making noise that carries.

## The burrow

Go in. The burrow is short, dark and occupied: a **ghoul** has claimed it, and the Sanzhana would like its home back. The fight is close-quarters — narrow tunnel, no room to kite — so lead with your heaviest opener and use the tunnel's corners rather than its middle. Vampiric claws make short work of ghouls; a sword and shield works fine if the sun is up.

When the ghoul is down, walk back to the burrow's mouth and report to the **Sanzhana**. The task closes, the Sanzhana is satisfied, and the tally toward your extra day ticks up: **one of four**.

## Why this one first

All four Sanzhana tasks (*Following the Sound*, *Sorrow of the Burrow*, *Pocket Dancer*, *Pearly and Precious*) unlock together after *Good Home*, and any order works. This one earns its slot early for two reasons: it's the shortest, and its starting shrine — the Mud Cup Tavern — sits on a road you'll use constantly. Bank it, then pick up the rest on your existing routes rather than making special trips.

When all four are done, the game still makes you **return to the Tsarina in person** to collect the bonus day — the single most-skipped step in the chain. Don't skip it; the day only lands when you report back.
