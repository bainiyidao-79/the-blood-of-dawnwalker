*Live Bait* is the prologue's fishing afternoon: Coen's younger siblings want to catch dinner, the wolves have them spooked, and a half-vampire brother is the perfect escort. It is a short, warm quest with one time-unit decision and a riverside conversation that quietly introduces the valley's politics.

## How to start

The quest branches off the ending of *Like Father, Like Son*: close that quest with **"See you then,"** and free time opens up. You'll find your younger siblings waiting on the path below the house — Pieter sent them to fish, but wolves on the prowl have them too nervous to go alone. Agree to come along; the trip costs **1–2 time units**.

## The walk to the river

- **The tag game.** On the path, the kids stall. You can spend **another time unit** playing tag — "let's play tag, that will get them running" — or tell them to quit complaining and push on. Neither choice changes the outcome; tag costs time, pushing on saves it.
- **More stalling at the river.** One more choice beat, both branches landing in the same place. Pick whichever fits your Coen.

## Baiting up

At the riverbank, use **Focus mode** to spot the **fishing rods** in the brush behind Yanna — there are four to collect. Set them up along the bank, bait each hook with a worm, and the scene rolls into the quest's real substance: conversation, first with the kids, then with strangers.

## The riverside conversations

- With the kids, the warm route runs: **"Anca is just a good friend of mine"**, then **"Because he's sad"**, then, baiting the hook — **"That will hurt, won't it?"**, and **"You don't like seeing animals suffer? Is that it?"**
- One of **Brencis's officers** interrupts. Answer him — **"As far as I know, he's still at home"** — and **"This has nothing to do with her."**
- When the **Urisash** man steps in and sends the officer off, close the scene warmly: **"People are just complaining"**, **"I'll pass it along honestly"**, and **"Thank you. I mean it."**
- After the Urisash leaves, the quiet closer: **"Maybe not all monsters are bad."**

Your picks here don't swing the story much, but the Urisash is doing you a favor — being decent to him costs nothing.

## Wrap-up and a free shrine

The quest pays out **100 XP**. On the way back — or as a detour while you're out — follow the map's shrine marker across the river and climb the rocks to unlock the **shrine above the marsh**. shrines are always worth the detour; this one opens a fast-travel node on the river's far bank.
