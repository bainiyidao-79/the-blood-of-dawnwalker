*Fate's Mercy* — listed as *Ambrus's Prisoners: Fate's Mercy* — is the rescue that runs off the Little Bridge shrine: Ambrus's prisoner transport has stopped in the trees, and the people in the cage have one very unusual rescuer coming through the fog. It's short, it pays a skill point, and one of the rescued is worth knowing for the rest of the run.

## How to start

Set out from the **Little Bridge shrine**, cross the bridge, and follow the road into the woods until you hit the transport: Ambrus's handlers and their chained cargo. The fight is straightforward — night makes it trivial — but mind the prisoners' position in the melee; stray swings cost you the people you came for.

## The choice after the fight

With the handlers down and the cage open, the freed prisoners ask what you want for it. The answer that defines the scene: **"I didn't save you for a reward."** It costs you a purse and earns the scene everything — this is one of the quests quietly measuring what kind of vampire Coen is becoming.

## Danilo

Among the rescued is a **blacksmith's apprentice** — a young man named Danilo, alive because Ambrus's men found him useful. He's worth a proper conversation before you all go your separate ways: forge-adjacent allies are rare in a valley where every anvil has a queue.

The quest completes on the walk out — **250 XP and 1 skill point**, plus the small, lasting satisfaction of having cost Ambrus a delivery. It will not be the last.

## The arc so far

Fate's Mercy slots into the Ambrus chain alongside *Roadside Surprise*, *Bounty of the Mire*, *Evil and Convenience* and the rest — each one a cut at the same network. Enough cuts and the network's owner takes an interest, which is how the arc's bigger rewards (and its duel) eventually find you.
