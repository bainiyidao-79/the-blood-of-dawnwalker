*Shadows in the Woods* is Mert's outside plan in motion: with the miller locked in the Stoneward Post, his ally **Sara** is waiting in the woods, and Mert wants Coen to make the introduction. Expect a bridge fight, a shrine worth fixing, one honest bribe, and an introduction to the most foul-mouthed ally in the valley.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/guides/shadows-in-the-woods-ambush.webp" alt="The daylight ambush near the Stoneward Post bridge" /><figcaption>The bridge outside the Stoneward Post — hold the crossing, then fix what the fight broke.</figcaption></div>

## How to start

The thread begins at the **bridge near the Stoneward Post**: a group of hostiles holds the crossing. Clear them, and while you're there — **repair the broken shrine** beside the bridge. It's free fast travel in a corner of the map you'll keep visiting for the whole Ambrus arc.

## The meeting point

Follow the marker into the trees to the meeting spot. A soldier questions what business you have here; the conversation runs:

1. **"That girl is my sister."**
2. When the toll comes up, pay it: **"No idea. But maybe these coins can answer that?"** — it costs **100 gold**, and it's the smooth way through.

## Sara

Talk to **Sara** and open with the password that matters: **"Mert sent me."** That line is the quest — she relaxes, and the woods open up.

## Crake's hideout

Sara leads you to the hideout, where **Crake** runs things. The conversation is a two-step dance:

1. Answer his framing question with **"A friend."**
2. Cycle through the white (repeatable) options until new lines appear, then pick **"That about covers it. That's what I did before."**
3. One more round of white options, then the closer: **"Has anyone ever told you what hard work you are?"**

That final jab lands the quest — Crake respects people who push back, and the hideout becomes a safe point in the woods for the rest of the arc. *Shadows in the Woods* pays **1,000 XP**, one of the fattest single-quest rewards in the early game.

With Mert's inside man and outside allies both in place, the jailbreak is now on the table: *Under Watchful Eyes* takes you back into the Stoneward Post for the breakout itself.
