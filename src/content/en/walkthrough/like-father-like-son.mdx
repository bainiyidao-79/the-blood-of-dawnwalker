*Like Father, Like Son* is the prologue's sword lesson: a short sparring session with Coen's father **Pieter** that ends with the **Charge skill manual** — a technique book that pays out in every sword fight that follows. It is short, cheap, and one of the prologue's must-do quests.

## How to start

After *Withering Away* wraps, Pieter asks Coen to follow him up the hill for training. Follow him to the training spot to begin the spar.

## Free loot on the way

Before or after the fight, check the **basket to the left of the house** — it holds **beef and mutton**, a free stack of cooking supplies for the days ahead. Prologue loot is modest; take it anyway.

## The spar

Beat Pieter in the practice bout. The fight is a tutorial for spacing and the game's directional combat — he hits hard for an early-game opponent but nothing here is punishing. Win the exchange to open the conversation.

## The dialogue after the fight

Three picks, and each is worth a thought:

1. **"This plan is madness. We can't win."** — the honest read of Pieter's idea.
2. **"Whatever you decide, I'm with you."** — the supportive answer; it's the one that keeps the scene warm.
3. **"See you then."** — closing on this line matters: it's the trigger that later opens the door to *Live Bait*, the fishing afternoon with your younger siblings.

## The reward

The quest completes with **100 XP and the Charge skill manual**, which teaches the Charge attack — the gap-closer you will lean on in every human-form fight from here to the endgame. If you are budgeting the prologue's time segments, this quest is cheap for what it returns; run it the same day as *Withering Away* and end the day with *Sacred Covenant*.
