*Where Old Demons Sleep* sends Coen and Anca into the valley's oldest ruins chasing the legend of a **fountain of life** — and it is mandatory for anyone courting Anca. The quest alternates treasure-riddle dialogue with a ruin dive full of wraiths, murals and one very specific door only Anca can open. Note: this page's English title follows the source quest name; an in-game spelling check is on our to-verify list.

## How to start

Find Anca at the **Crossroads Inn** and ask about her research. The white option — **"Thank you for always going all in to help me"** — opens the thread; when the new line appears, take **"Maybe, but you've done far more for me than that."** Keep pushing the conversation and the fountain-of-life search begins.

## The map riddle

Anca's notes want landmarks, and the game plays it as a map game:

1. Pick the point marked by the **two crosses** on the right side of the map — that's the fountain's first clue.
2. When she teases that there might be more surprises, take **"Maybe I've prepared more surprises for you."**
3. Then select the **lake point** on the right side of the map. That settles the destination.

## The dawn departure

The trip happens at **dawn** — find Anca at first light and set out together. At the lake, the conversation keeps courting:

- **"I know exactly that feeling you mean."**
- **"No — your business is my business."**
- On the braiding remark: **"Ribbons weaving into shape… the way a woman braids her hair?"**
- When the new option appears, take **"Yes — right in front of us"**, then choose the **second option**: follow the stream upstream to the spring.

## The ruins

The stream delivers you to the ruins. At the overlook, take the warm lines — **"It's good to see you smile"** and **"I'm glad I came with you"** — then deal with the **watchdogs** below and enter by the **right-hand entrance**.

Inside:

1. Clear the **wraith** from the first chamber and loot its chest.
2. **Examine the mural** on the chamber wall — the ruin's story starts there.
3. Back out and down the stairs, clear the lower room, and reach the **fountain door**. When Anca despairs, answer **"Don't give up. There has to be a way."**
4. Investigate the **broken pot and stone rod** on the stone table to the left, then the **mural** on the right.

## The rite

The murals spell out the rite: the door answers to paint, not keys. Let **Anca paint the marks on her skin**, and the ancient door opens. The quest completes at the threshold — and the next quest, *Beyond Achilles*, picks up on the other side with everything the fountain has been hiding.
