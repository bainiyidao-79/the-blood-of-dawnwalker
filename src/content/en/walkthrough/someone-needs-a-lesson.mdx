*Someone Needs a Lesson* is the prologue's local-bully quest: Vasil has been making trouble for Coen's family, and this quest lets you chase him down and settle it — with words, fists, or an ill-advised second fight with his father. One time unit in, 250 XP and a decent conversation out.

## How to start

While waiting for mass in Laslea Glen, walk to the **shrine at the center of the village**, then to the **door northeast of the shrine** — approaching it triggers the quest automatically as Vasil makes his move.

## The chase

Vasil runs, and you chase him across the village. Catch up and grab him for a "talk." The confrontation unfolds in three beats, and every branch of it is survivable:

1. **"Why would you do that?"** — open with the confrontation.
2. **"Take that back."** — push the point.
3. **"I've had enough"** — settle it with a **duel**.

## Beat Vasil, then handle Atanas

Defeat Vasil in the fight and his father **Atanas** steps in to negotiate. The conversation branches:

- **De-escalate:** "We just had a small disagreement," then "Huh? All right," then **"Let him go, Atanas."** — the clean route; Vasil gets his beating from his own father and the quest closes without further blood.
- **The drunk-fight route:** **"You're drunk, Atanas"** — and you get a second duel against the father. Someone is getting pounded into a pulp either way; you're just choosing who.

Afterward, meet Vasil again and close with **"I'm sorry."** The quest completes for **250 XP**, plus the small story note that Vasil and his father have troubles of their own.

## Is it worth the time unit?

Yes — it's one of the better XP-per-minute deals in the prologue, it fills in the village's texture, and neither branch carries a lasting penalty. Do it during the mass-wait window alongside *Blasphemy*, *On the Run*, and *Disturbed*; that cluster of small quests is the most efficient hour in the game's opening days.
