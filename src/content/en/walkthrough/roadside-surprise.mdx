*Roadside Surprise* — full title *Ambrus's Transports: Roadside Surprise* — is the first thread of the Ambrus arc that runs across the whole valley: one man's logistics network of prisoners, leeches and loot, strung between the swamp and the mountains. This segment starts right outside the Stoneward Post and takes about ten minutes.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/guides/roadside-surprise-convoy.webp" alt="Ambrus's overturned convoy by the roadside" /><figcaption>Ambrus's convoy, parked permanently — the first link of his supply chain you'll be cutting.</figcaption></div>

## How to start

From the **Stoneward Post shrine**, walk to the camp on the right side of the road. The camp is hostile — clear the fighters first, then loot.

## What to pick up

- **Search the bodies.** One carries a **letter about the cargo** — read it; it names the operation you've just interrupted and points at the rest of Ambrus's network.
- **The urgent mail** sits on a barrel. Collecting it starts a connected errand toward **Ambrus's leech collectors** — the arc's next threads (*Wetland Helpers*, *Bounty of the Mire* and friends).
- Sweep the camp's containers before moving on; this arc pays in potions and denarii.

## Burn the barrels

In the middle of the camp stands a stack of **wooden barrels** — Ambrus's goods. Destroy them. It's one animation, it denies the convoy its cargo, and it's the quest's actual objective dressed as property damage.

## Brave Klaus

As the camp burns, you'll meet **Brave Klaus** — a would-be recruit having a very bad day of it. The kind ending is the correct one: **"Come on, don't give up. One day you'll earn its favor."** Encourage him and the quest closes — *Ambrus's Transports: Roadside Surprise* complete, **250 XP** on the tally.

## Why this arc matters

Every Ambrus quest you complete chips at the same network — transports, collectors, dungeons, cellars — and enough interference eventually makes the man himself look up from his ledger. That confrontation is the arc's payoff: beat him and his gear is yours, along with a note that starts one of the valley's stranger quests. This first camp is where that road starts.
