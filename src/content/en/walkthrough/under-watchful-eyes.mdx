*Under Watchful Eyes* is the payoff of the Stoneward Post arc: at night, with the guard rotation thin, Coen goes over the walls and takes Mert out of Brencis's prison by force. It is the most combat-dense of the post quests — boars, towers, a yard-to-yard clear — and a showcase for everything vampiric traversal can do.

## How to start

Wait for **nightfall**, then start from the shrine on the common outside the post. The whole quest is tuned for darkness: your powers are at full strength, and half the garrison is asleep.

## The way in

- Follow the road toward the **distress-call marker** on the compass.
- The detour on the way: a villager has a **boar problem**. Clear the beasts, choose **"You're welcome"** when thanked, and pocket the **gold and XP**. Good deeds near this post pay literal interest.
- Continue northeast to the **tower** at the wall's corner. This is the entry: use your vampiric climb to go up the outside, then **Shadowstep through the window** to the far side. The wall exists for people without claws.

## Freeing Mert

Drop down inside, quietly remove the guards around the cell block, and break **Mert** out. The miller's part in this arc ends here — with the post's own keys jingling in his pocket on the way past.

## Fighting out

The exit is the hard part, and the quest stages it as three clearances:

1. Up the stairs, **the watch commander** — a named fighter with real health. Spend your Shadowstep charges on approach angles and don't trade in the open.
2. Loot the room, then out the door into the **second yard** — another patrol group, then the gate.
3. The last stretch holds the **post's warden and his guards**. Everything you've learned about night combat applies: claws over swords, bites on cooldown, never let them surround you.

Clear the final yard, walk out the gate, and *Under Watchful Eyes* completes for **250 XP** — with Mert safe, Sara's hideout expecting him, and the Stoneward Post quietly counting its dead. The Ambrus arc continues around the post's edges: *Evil and Convenience* is the next thread to pull.
