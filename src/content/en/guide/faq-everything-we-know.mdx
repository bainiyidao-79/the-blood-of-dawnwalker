Quick answers to the questions players search most, grounded in official materials and launch-window coverage. Updated September 1, 2026 — one day before launch.

## Is The Blood of Dawnwalker Multiplayer?

**No.** The Blood of Dawnwalker is a **single-player** game — every official material and preview describes a solo experience, and no co-op or multiplayer modes have been announced.

## Is It on Game Pass?

**Not announced.** No Game Pass inclusion has been confirmed. (The game does support **Xbox Play Anywhere** — one digital purchase covers Xbox console and PC — and **Xbox Cloud Gaming** is confirmed.)

## Will It Run on Steam Deck?

**No official verdict yet.** Valve hasn't published a Steam Deck rating at the time of writing. The official minimum specs (GTX 1060, 16 GB RAM, SSD) are above Deck class, and Rebel Wolves hasn't made Deck claims — treat any "Deck verified" chatter as speculation until Valve's rating lands.

## Is It Coming to Nintendo Switch?

**Not announced.** No Switch or Switch 2 version has been confirmed.

## Is There Romance?

**Yes.** Romance has been confirmed with multiple romancible characters — including **Anka**, a witch with early-game flirty dialogue options, and **Lacram**, a route noted for a divergent story outcome. The twist: romance quests and intimate scenes **advance the in-game clock** — sometimes by a full day-night cycle — so relationships are a time-investment decision like everything else.

## How Many Endings Are There?

Rebel Wolves hasn't published an ending count. What's confirmed: it's a **narrative sandbox** where quests can be failed, ignored or destroyed, the 30-day clock can run out **without a game-over screen** (the family is lost and the story continues down a darker branch), and previewers have teased **hidden/secret endings**. Multiple endings are strongly implied by the structure; the exact count is a launch-window reveal.

## How Big Is the Map?

**Deliberately smaller than genre giants — and denser.** Vale Sangora is a handcrafted Carpathian valley built on "density over scale": no tower-climbing checklist filler, quests and characters that react as time passes, shrines, vantage-point scouting, and buried treasure maps. Game director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz has been openly critical of giant, unknowable open worlds.

## Who Do You Play As?

**Coen** — a young man turned into a **Dawnwalker** (human by day, vampire by night) after a botched transformation caused by the silver dust in his body. His family has been taken by the vampire ruler **Brencis**, and he has 30 days to save them.

## Who Makes The Blood of Dawnwalker?

**Rebel Wolves** — a studio founded by veteran **CD Projekt Red** developers, led by former Witcher 3 director **Konrad Tomaszkiewicz**. Published by **Bandai Namco Entertainment**, built on **Unreal Engine 5**. Rebel Wolves has also teased the **Dawnwalker saga** as a long-term franchise spanning different historical eras — though this game is strictly set in the 14th century.

## How Much Does It Cost?

**$69.99** (Standard), with Eclipse digital, physical Day 1, and limited Collector's editions (23 cm Coen figurine, hardcover compendium, world map). Full breakdown in the [Price & Editions guide](/release/price-and-editions).

## What About Reviews and Performance?

Reviews began landing around launch (the review embargo lifted as stores went live) — Metacritic and OpenCritic pages for the game are already up and accumulating scores. On performance: PC preview builds were solid, and a **day-one patch** adds a console performance mode **targeting 60 FPS** on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X. We'll keep this page updated as launch data settles.
