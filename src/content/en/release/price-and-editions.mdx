## The Blood of Dawnwalker Price

The Blood of Dawnwalker is a full-priced AAA release: **$69.99** on both PC (Steam) and PS5 (US pricing, checked September 1, 2026 — regional pricing varies).

## Editions

Four editions have been officially detailed:

| Edition | Format | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard Edition** | Digital | Base game |
| **Eclipse Edition** | Digital | Base game + **Digital World Compendium** + **Digital Comic Book** + **Digital Soundtrack** |
| **Day 1 Edition** | Physical | Base game + **SteelBook** + physical **world map** + pre-order armor set |
| **Collector's Edition** | Physical, limited | **23 cm PureArts Coen figurine** + **60-page hardcover World Compendium** + **33×40 cm world map** + SteelBook + all Eclipse Edition digital extras |

Collector's Edition pricing varies by retailer and region — check your local store listing. There is no gameplay-advantage content in any edition: everything above is cosmetic, collectible or lore material.

## Pre-Order Bonus

Pre-ordering any edition unlocks the **Sangoran Wayfarer's Armor Set** — an outfit bearing the emblem of a nameless travelling smith.

Important: this is an **early unlock, not a permanent exclusive**. The armor can also be earned later through normal progression, so pre-ordering saves time rather than locking content away from non-pre-order players.

## Which Edition Makes Sense?

- **Standard** — the complete game, nothing paywalled
- **Eclipse (digital)** — worth it if you want the lore compendium, comic and OST
- **Day 1 (physical)** — the collectible route for console players who want the SteelBook and printed map
- **Collector's** — for figurine collectors; the PureArts Coen statue and the hardcover compendium are the headline items
