## Release Date: September 3, 2026

The Blood of Dawnwalker launches on **September 3, 2026** for PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That's the official global date — multiple publisher and retail listings agree on it.

**Why do some store pages say September 2?** The Steam listing shows a September 2 unlock in some regions — that's a time-zone artifact of the same global launch moment (a game unlocking the evening of Sep 2 in US time is already Sep 3 in Europe and Asia). Your launcher will simply unlock the game when it's midnight somewhere local, so check your own region's store page for the exact hour.

## Console Performance: The Day-One Patch

Early console footage showed 30 FPS modes (up to 40 on PS5 Pro / Series X), which raised questions. Rebel Wolves has since confirmed a **day-one patch** adding a **performance mode targeting 60 FPS** on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X — note the word *targeting*. Quality and fidelity modes are also included.

On PC, preview builds were described as solid for frame rate and loading, and early hands-on reports across desktops and handhelds reported no major issues — full launch performance data lands with reviews.
