## What is The Blood of Dawnwalker?

**The Blood of Dawnwalker** is an open-world dark fantasy action RPG developed by **Rebel Wolves** — a studio founded by veteran **CD Projekt Red** developers and led by former Witcher 3 director **Konrad Tomaszkiewicz** — and published by **Bandai Namco Entertainment**, built on **Unreal Engine 5**. It launches on **September 3, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC** (Steam and GOG).

## The Premise: 30 Days and Nights

You play as **Coen**, a young man from 14th-century Europe who is turned into a vampire — but not completely. Years of silver-mine work left silver dust in his body, and it interfered with the transformation. The result: Coen is a **Dawnwalker** — **human by day, vampire by night**.

After the vampire ruler **Brencis** takes Coen's family, Coen has **30 days and nights** to rescue them and disrupt the vampire regime. Crucially, time is not a real-time countdown — it's a **resource you spend** on quests, training and major choices. Spending it in one place means running out of it somewhere else.

## The Setting: Vale Sangora

The story unfolds in **Vale Sangora**, a fictional Carpathian valley in an alternative 14th-century Europe devastated by war and the Black Death. Ancient vampires have emerged from the shadows and overthrown the old feudal rulers — and the moral picture is deliberately gray: under vampire rule, people are protected from the plague, but they pay their new masters in blood.

## The Narrative Sandbox

Rebel Wolves calls the structure a **narrative sandbox**: after the prologue there is no traditional main-quest/side-quest split. You can pursue, ignore, interrupt or outright destroy storylines — tackle regions in any order, kill important characters, or even rush straight to Brencis (which the devs say is near-impossible, but they won't stop you). Fail a quest, and the story doesn't collapse — it bends toward a darker version of itself.
